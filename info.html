How it works

StegApp is a browser front end. The pages you see are served from one host, while the actual steganography runs on a backend that has the command-line tools installed.

  • steghide — embeds and extracts hidden payloads, and is what the Embed and Extract pages drive.
  • stegseek — a fast cracker used by the Detect and Crack pages to test for hidden data and recover passphrases from a wordlist.
  • Uploads and results are processed on the backend and are not stored permanently; restarting the backend clears them.
